Emojitsu

Privacy Policy · Effective October 4, 2026

Short version: Emojitsu does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The game does not use the internet.

1. Who we are

Emojitsu (package com.emojitsu.game) is a single-player game made by Biseo Studio. Contact: efdou100@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

None. The app has no account system, contains no advertising or analytics SDKs, and does not request the Internet permission. We never receive your name, email, location, device identifiers, contacts, photos or any other personal data.

3. Data stored on your device

Your game progress (discovered spells, run in progress, best scores and settings such as sound and language) is saved only on your device using standard app storage. It is never sent to us or anyone else. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it permanently.

4. Purchases

Emojitsu is sold through Google Play. Payment is processed entirely by Google under the Google Privacy Policy. As the developer we may receive limited transaction information from Google (such as order number, price and country) solely for accounting and refunds. We do not receive your payment card details.

5. Sharing

When you tap “Share” on the results screen, the text you choose to share is passed to the app you pick (for example a messenger). Emojitsu itself does not send it anywhere.

6. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

7. Your rights and deletion

Because we hold no personal data, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. All game data lives on your device and can be removed by uninstalling the app or via Android Settings › Apps › Emojitsu › Storage › Clear data.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above. Significant changes will also be noted in the app’s release notes.
